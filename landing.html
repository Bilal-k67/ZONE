How a session runs

THREE STEPS, NO STAFF NEEDED

1

Scan the station

Every PC and console has its own code taped to the desk. Scanning it opens that machine's session page — not anyone else's.

2

Play, timer runs

The session starts the moment you confirm. A running total shows on screen so there's never a surprise at checkout.

3

Walk out, it's settled

End the session and the balance is deducted automatically. Your visit is logged toward your next reward.
